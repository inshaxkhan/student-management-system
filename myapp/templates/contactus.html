Which board is Seth Anandram Jaipuria School, Lucknow, affiliated to?

Our school is affiliated to the following boards/curricula: CBSE Curriculum: This curriculum is offered for learners of all age groups (All Classes) The school has introduced the International Early Years Curriculum (IEYC) in the academic year 2024-25. The IEYC is for pre-primary learners. IEYC is for children aged between 3 to 6+ years. It covers all areas of learning relevant to the early years, kindergarten and pre-school.


Is there an entrance exam for seeking admission?

Admission in Seth Anandram Jaipuria School, Lucknow is purely based on seat availability. However, siblings of existing children would be given priority. The process is online. A registration form needs to be filled along with uploading of documents (as mentioned in the form). A registration fee of Rs.1500 is to be paid online to complete the process.


What is the school’s approach to education?

We aim at holistic development where students are taught the way they want to learn. We follow child-centric learning and it is a learning-by-doing approach as well as experiential. We empower, enthuse and make children excel by nurturing them to become lifelong learners.
