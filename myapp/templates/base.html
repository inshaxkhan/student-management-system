ABOUT US


Seth Anandram Jaipuria School, Sadhu Campus – Barabanki, follows the motto ‘Empower, Enthuse and Excel’ by sparking the students’ curiosity and enthusing and empowering them with knowledge to excel in all aspects of school education.
